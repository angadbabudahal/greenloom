What is Greenloom?

Greenloom is a Nepali brand dedicated entirely to hemp.

Born in Nepal, Greenloom was created with a simple vision — to bring the natural versatility of hemp into modern everyday living through thoughtfully designed products.

From clothing to everyday essentials, we work exclusively with hemp, celebrating its natural character, comfort, durability, and timeless appeal.

We believe hemp is more than a fabric. It is a material with a story, a connection to nature, and the potential to shape a more conscious way of creating and consuming.

At Greenloom, we combine the heritage of hemp with contemporary design to create products that are simple, functional, distinctive, and made to last.

Our ambition is to build Nepal’s leading hemp brand and introduce the quality and possibilities of Nepali hemp to customers around the world.
